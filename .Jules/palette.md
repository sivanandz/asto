## 2024-05-18 - Missing tooltips on icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Found several icon-only buttons (like `IconButton` for back or clear text) missing `tooltip` labels in the application. This causes accessibility issues for screen readers and makes it harder for users to identify the button's action.
**Action:** Adding tooltips to icon buttons, especially those that are used for navigation or clearing text, is essential for a complete and accessible user experience.
